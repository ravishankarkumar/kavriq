## Mathematics

Fractals, patterns, probability, chaos, geometry, and other ideas that are best understood by playing with them.

[Fractals](/fun/mathematics/fractals) follows a copying rule until a curve, a triangle, or a tree appears. [Hidden order](/fun/mathematics/hidden-order) looks at the shapes that fall out of noise, out of an orbit that runs away, and out of the next word in a sentence.
